Hackathon Basics

Here is how today works.▋

Keep it simple, move fast, and demo what you made.
01
Build anything with AI
There is no set prompt. Pick any idea where AI helps solve, summarize, automate, generate, or explain something.
02
Teams of 4
Work in teams of up to four. Split roles fast: idea, build, test, and demo.
03
One-hour sprint
Aim to have something demoable by 11:15. Rough but working beats perfect but unfinished.
Rule of thumb: if you can explain the idea in one sentence, you can probably build a version of it today.

What to Build

Start with a tiny problem.▋

Then use AI to make it faster, smarter, easier, or more fun.
01
Pick a task
Something annoying, repetitive, confusing, slow, manual, or boring.
02
Add AI
Use chat, agents, copilots, prompts, code, data, summaries, generation, or automation.
03
Show the impact
Demo the before and after. What changed because AI was involved?
A good demo does not need to be huge. It just needs to make the room understand the idea.
